Little Letter Garden

Privacy for parents and caregivers

Effective 23 September 2026

Little Letter Garden is a phonics and first-word game for young children. This policy explains how the Android app handles information. For questions, contact the developer at collinsdaryldev@gmail.com.

Information the app uses

The app does not ask for a child's name, email address, birthday, location, camera or microphone access. It has no accounts, advertising, analytics, tracking or in-app purchases. The app does not collect or share personal data.

Learning progress, the current word and the music setting are stored on the device so play can continue later. These settings are not sent to us. Android backup is disabled. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes these settings.

Audio and internet

Pictures, music, sound effects and recorded voice clips are included with the app. They play without a network connection. If a recording cannot be played, the app may ask the device's installed text-to-speech engine to read a short, fixed learning prompt. That engine is supplied by the device provider and follows its own settings and privacy terms; the app does not provide it with personal information.

Children's privacy

The app is intended for young children to use with a parent or caregiver. It does not offer social features, user-generated content or links inside the child activities. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions or requests, email collinsdaryldev@gmail.com. If this policy changes, the updated version and effective date will appear here.
